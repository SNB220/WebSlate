Install WebSlate

Keep a calm writing and coding workspace one click away from GitHub or any other page.

  1. Show your browser's bookmarks bar.
  2. Drag the green WebSlate bookmarklet button onto it.
  3. Click that bookmark whenever you want to open WebSlate.
WebSlate bookmarklet

If dragging is unavailable, copy the URL above, create a new bookmark, and paste it into the bookmark's URL field. Your files stay in this browser.
